# Presentation & Demo Plan

## Target Audience
Hospital Administrators, Department Heads, and IT Staff.

## Flow

### 1. Introduction 
- Brief overview of the Seshat AI - Hospital Management System.
- Highlighting the pain points: missed appointments, inefficient bed allocation, and manual data interpretation.

### 2. Data Overview Dashboard 
- Showcase the realistic synthetic data generation.
- Demonstrate the live tracking of patient records and incoming appointments.

### 3. Predictive Analytics in Action 
- Navigate to the Predictive Analytics tab.
- Demonstrate the No-Show Prediction model. Select a department and show how the pre-trained Random Forest model instantly categorizes the risk level without any lag.

### 4. Resource Optimization 
- Navigate to the Resource Optimization tab.
- Show the prediction of inpatient lengths of stay across various wards (e.g., ICU vs. General), emphasizing how this enables proactive capacity planning.

### 5. AI Insights
- Navigate to the AI Insights tab.
- Click "Generate Hospital Insights" to trigger the LLM agent.
- Read through the generated narrative recommendations to show how the system bridges the gap between raw statistics and actionable administrative strategies.
